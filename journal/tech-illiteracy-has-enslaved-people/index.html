Tech Illiteracy Has Enslaved People

Rosich Marcos

2025/06/27

People went from knowing how to survive in the wilderness to sacrificing every piece of knowledge that doesn’t add up to their careers.

By 2030 there will be no more programmers, no creators, no designers, no one will know how to do anything.

We will only have promters, AI generated content that complies with ‘ze guidelines’ and consoomers watching ads every 30 seconds.

Wisdom has enabled humanity to multiply production exponentially with higher levels of technlogic complexity, but the thing with technology getting more and more complex is that they are now just black boxes to the average human.

As a result, we live in a world in which we don’t know how anything works.

The world is moving increasingly to the digital space. The virtual world has overtaken the real one.

In digital space, humans are being enslaved by tech corporations everyday because they are lazy (or they are too tired thanks to their life-sucking job / career)

Does the average human know how to properly install an adblocker? Do they even know e-mail exists beyond Gmail, Outlook and iCloud?

Normal people aren´t aware of the Terms and Conditions they blindly accept. They are willing to give every last bit of freedom just to consume more.

10 years ago, kids didn’t know what a .txt was, now they don’t know how to use a computer. Thanks Apple!

By 2030 there will be no more programmers, no creators, no designers. We will only have prompters, AI generated content and consoomers watching ads every 30 seconds.

Freedom is the state of not being enslaved. Freedom is the power to do whatever you want.

A human being will never be in a complete state of freedom because we are limited in the amount of wisdom we can produce and possess

Only god can know it all because He is wisdom itself. We on the other hand are slaves of our own ignorance

People went from knowing how to survive in the wilderness to sacrificing every piece of knowledge that doesn’t add up to their careers.

There is people in OpenAI that grew up with iMacs and don’t know how to build a PC. And what would they need to know? Maybe they are more free just not having to know that

just consume product and be happy!
