I Have Published My First Project on Google Play Store

Rosich Marcos

2025/06/13

This year, I set out to launch a project on the Google Play Store.

For the past few years, new developers have had to go through a rigorous testing process that requires involving 14 people to try out an app before it can be published. Although at first the process felt tedious (as it prevented me from finishing the project quickly), in retrospect I can say that the result of the testing process allowed me to deliver a more polished and professional app.

It was a great practice experience, and this game marks my first published project on a platform with massive reach.

I’m happy to share it!
